App Lock Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

App Lock ("App Lock", "we", "our", or "the App") is an Android application developed by Mr. Good Will. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use App Lock.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, App Lock may process information necessary to provide security functionality, including application configuration, security settings, authentication-related information, and information required to send requested security notifications.

When you voluntarily connect a Google Account, the application may receive basic Google Account information necessary for the OAuth connection, such as the account email address.

App Lock does not request, receive or store your Google Account password.

3. Gmail API Usage

App Lock provides an optional Gmail integration. If you choose to connect Gmail, App Lock uses the Google Gmail API with the gmail.send permission.

The Gmail permission is used to send security-related emails on your behalf, including OTP verification messages and security alerts.

App Lock does not use the Gmail integration to:

  • Read your Gmail inbox
  • Search your Gmail messages
  • Read the contents of your existing emails
  • Modify existing emails
  • Delete emails

4. Google OAuth

Google OAuth is used to securely authenticate and authorize your Google Account connection.

Your Google Account password is handled by Google and is never provided to or stored by App Lock.

You can revoke App Lock's access to your Google Account through your Google Account security settings.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed by App Lock may be used to:

  • Provide app-locking and security features
  • Perform requested OTP verification
  • Send security notifications
  • Maintain application functionality
  • Provide customer support

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

When you enable Gmail integration, App Lock communicates with Google services through Google's APIs to provide the email functionality you requested.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can choose whether to connect your Google Account and whether to use Gmail-based security notifications.

You may also revoke Google Account access at any time through your Google Account settings.

9. Children's Privacy

App Lock is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the application's functionality.

10. Third-Party Services

App Lock may use Google services, including Google OAuth and Gmail API, to provide optional functionality. Your use of Google services is subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
